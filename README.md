## 📌 Executive Summary & Real-World Impact

Delhi generates **11,000 metric tons of municipal solid waste daily**. Over **60% remains unsegregated at source**, going directly to overfilled landfills like Ghazipur and Bhalswa, causing methane fires, air pollution, and groundwater contamination.

Meanwhile, a large network of **20,000+ informal recyclers** recovers 20-25% of recyclables but operates without digital infrastructure, standardized pricing, and direct connectivity with households.

**BinWise** solves this through:

1.  **2-Stage Computer Vision Classifier:** Instant waste classification from camera photos and uploads, categorized into **Stage 1 (Biodegradable vs Non-Biodegradable)** and **Stage 2 (Sub-material: Plastic, Paper, Metal, Cardboard, E-Waste, Organic)**.
2.  **Recycling & Compost Network:** Directly connects citizens to verified local recyclers with digital market rates (Plastic ₹15/kg, Iron ₹34/kg) and decentralized compost plants.
3.  **AI Reuse Assistant:** Suggests creative, zero-cost upcycling ideas and calculates scrap earnings. Powered by Llama LLM API.
